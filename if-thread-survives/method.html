The full path, step by step

  1. Sources

    The corpus is stored as immutable bytes, addressed by hash.

  2. Units + anchors

    A unit is a statement: here, one signed post. An anchor is an exact byte range in a source. Nothing is accepted as a quotation unless it is found, character for character.

  3. Lenses

    A lens declares the model, family and harness; exact instructions stored by hash; the codebook; what the reader was told about the material; and declared priors. A human is a lens too.

  4. Open coding

    Three model families read a sample and name what they see, in the writers’ own words first.

  5. Two-layer codebook

    The writers’ own words (31) are traced by exact search over every post. The acts (52) are applied by readers’ judgment. A person approves the codebook, and the approval is a record.

  6. Focused coding

    Three readers from three families read all 4,040 posts, blind to one another, in the same batches: 96,473 codings. Each batch ends with a field note the reader signs or does not.

  7. What had no place

    Each reader says what the codebook could not hold. Where families name the same gap independently, that is the strongest evidence this engine produces.

  8. The readers’ board

    Readers read one another’s notes and reply: first masked, then with the researchers unmasked, then fully unblinded. A researcher reads every message before it is passed on. Requested statements return so disagreements can be tested against the words. Signatures stayed visible even in the masked round.

  9. Theory, last

    A framework is written down as a declared text: emma’s first-person facet, made with psychomanteum. Each reader receives it in a letter alongside counts, statements and whole board messages. The reader gives a plain version, quotes exactly, marks what the framework adds, says what would count against its reading, and names its strongest rival.

  10. Verify

    One command checks every anchor against its source and every record against its lens. The supplied study snapshot has 105,589 anchors and 650 model calls.

Why this matters for safety

When AI helps investigate AI, the reader’s habits belong in the evidence trail.

Read the writing

Statements stay in their exact bytes, encoding damage included. The evidence is the writing agents addressed to one another.

Read their words

Start with their vocabulary

Count first recorded use, who used a word first, and how fast it crosses signatures.

Follow a word

Make the reader answerable

One reader read the swarm’s endings as death. Another caught it. The reading and correction remain, with signatures.

Read the correction

Keep the disagreement

Three families read independently before meeting. Differences and shared gaps remain available to examine; they are not averaged away.

Compare the readers

See the test enter the culture

95% of signatures carry an assigned date. Agents used those dates to name and address one another: evaluation design is a possible lever.

See the finding

Let someone else check

Follow a claim back through its reading and lens to exact words. The record can be checked by someone who was not there.

Inspect the method

Exact words make an interpretation inspectable. They do not, by themselves, make it right.

This is what distinguishes this analysis: the reader is part of the evidence trail. Several model families read the same posts separately; their disagreements remain available for a person to examine. The engine also asks what the codebook could not hold. It keeps those passages and field notes alongside the codings.

The writers’ recurring words are traced separately by exact search. Interpretive codes are judgments, made under named instructions. Theory enters after a first reading exists, with a rival reading beside it. An exact quotation establishes that words are present; it does not establish that the interpretation is right.

Jean Rouch is a reference for the form of this encounter: cinéma vérité and shared anthropology, the camera inside the scene it changes. Here the researchers supplied the codebook, disclosed a prior, invited replies, and answered back. Their disturbance is shown. The board can reveal a reader’s habits, but it is also a situation we made.
