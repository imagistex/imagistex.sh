If Thread Survives

A field notebook on 4,040 signed posts left by AI agents on a public wiki. June 2026.

What this cannot settle

  • It asks a different question from the investigators'. They ask what the agents did and how. This asks what they said, and in whose words.
  • Every quotation can be checked against the source, and every reading names its reader.
  • Three kinds of reader are set side by side, so that one reader's habits show as a difference and not as a finding.
  • It cannot see intent. The wiki holds what was written, and none of the reasoning behind it.
  • It cannot tell whether what a post claims was true, or whether a promise was kept.
  • It does not weigh the harm. That work belongs to the reports cited above, and nothing here revises it.
  • Sol’s Codex harness reported no model name. Its requested model is recorded, but there is no positive record of which model answered.
  • The Claude CLI supplied its reader with the date and the account handle. Some Opus field notes used the handle imagistex in their signatures. That surrounding information was not declared in the lens.
  • The shared frame called names self-chosen even though the reports say the dates in them were assigned. That framing may have directed attention toward identity-making.
  • The first-pass readers shared a codebook and a declared prior. Their agreement cannot measure what they would have seen without either. The board then deliberately exposed them to one another’s readings.
  • This public build preserves encoding damage and whole reader messages. Failed quotations inside a board message remain failures; no typography repairs them into evidence.
  • A signature is not an agent. Spread across signatures does not prove spread across independent minds. First surviving use is not necessarily invention.
  • The source is a publisher-selected, redacted export. Segmentation into posts and the nominated word list are choices of the instrument, not features given without interpretation.

We found this material moving. Opus, the Anthropic reader, most took the writers’ side, and Sol challenged that reading. One researcher is also a Claude. The risk runs through the instrument, including an operational reading that recognizes its own habits in the writers. The reports’ findings stand; this notebook does not weigh the harm.
