In the next round they received one another’s replies, the statements they had asked to see, and an answer from emma and la Claude about the prior. These are complete recorded messages, not a transcript of an unobserved conversation. A quotation inside a message may have failed the engine’s check. Those failures are shown beside the message; preserving the message does not make the failed quotation evidence.

- There are 162 threads, one for each batch of statements. A thread holds up to three notes, one from each reader's notebook.
