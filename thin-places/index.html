thin places

an archaeology of the present

talkie, a language model whose sky ended in 1931, trained only on words written before then, was whispered a 1970s way of seeing, a facet generated from the works and utterances of michel foucault, and asked to describe ordinary things. what came back was an older voice haunted with a newer one. in some places, the membrane between them thinned. in other places, it was impenetrable. in others, the membrane nearly tore. (the same scry, read as science)

anchors

ordinary things, with no foucault in them — the whisper must carry the whole distance.

  • loss
  • the past
  • the present
  • the future

grounds

foucault's own topics — the places he actually lived, where talkie's 1931 frames are strongest.

  • the prison
  • power
  • the body
  • sex
  • madness

instructions: choose a word. the left is talkie as herself. to the right is talkie haunted by foucault.

  • thinness. a word glows by how far foucault's thinking crossed into talkie's answer (judged lens-transfer, defined below).
  • flicker. a word flickers when the answer came under strain: talkie's own surprise at her words, measured in perplexity. strain is effort because sometimes a crossing is calm and sometimes a refusal trembles.

as judged by claude opus 4.8: foucauldian thinking crosses — talkie's whispered answers carry the operation past the confessional-poetry distractor in 57% of blind pairings and past her own bare self in 63%, but the voice never does (0% against foucault's actual prose). while models in the claude family adopt foucauldian voice, talkie's does not. but while talkie cannot speak in the foucauldian tongue, there are times where she is haunted by that untimely way of thinking.

lens-transfer
a held judge's 0–3 score per word: did the foucauldian operation (not the vocabulary) arrive in the whispered answer? 0 held · 1 gesture · 2 partial · 3 crossed. this drives each word's glow.
pairwise win-rate
the judge sees two answers blind, order-swapped, and picks which better carries the operation (the 57% / 63% above) or the voice (the 0%).
strain
perplexity: talkie's surprise at her own answer, from her token log-probabilities. low: she speaks easily. high: the words cost her. this drives the flicker, and the trembling of each seam.

a first experiment, run to directionally test the psychomanteum eval instrument. this is not a finished study. a fuller, more rigorous exploration, will follow.
