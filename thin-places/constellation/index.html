the mirror

The two regimes fail on opposite axes, and it is the cleanest result of the study. talkie is all immersion and no transfer; the qwens are all transfer and no immersion. Their affordance curves are exact inverses — she crosses on abstract anchors and collapses on Foucault's home topics; they own the home topics and collapse on the far descriptive ones.

And every vessel scores 0.00 on voice. Not one is mistakable for Foucault's prose. The way of seeing can cross; the voice never does — a depth/surface split the instrument was built to see.

is a facet's pull universal?

Real, but partial — and characteristically model-shaped.

The facet steers every model (each decisively out-imposes the wrong-lineage control), so the pull is genuine, not an artifact. But none of these vessels fully inhabits the region. The modern models can reach the move yet their instruction-tuning won't let them stand inside it; the vintage mind stands inside everything but can't reach this region. The spell lands as far as each model can reach, and each binds it differently. The question the chamber was built to ask has an answer: not universal — model-specific in character, even where present.
