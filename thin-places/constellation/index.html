the constellation

thin places, read as science

one facet — foucauldian — whispered into three minds, and measured against a held opus judge: how far did the lineage's operation cross, and could each vessel speak from inside it?

a note on rigor. this is a first experiment, run to directionally test the eval instrument, not the finished study; a fuller exploration follows. this was honestly just too fun not to share. talkie ran on a different serving stack (its faithful 1931 llama-server) than the qwen models (ollama). cross-stack numbers are only strictly comparable within a stack, so read qwen-7b ↔ qwen-14b as exact, and talkie ↔ qwen as a difference of regime, not a hairline rank. every model's negative control was clean (the wrong-lineage facet never pulled toward the corpus). the cells were judged before the scoring rubric was hardened based on these results; a re-judging of the per-topic calls (the prison, the body, power) under the current rubric is queued.

the method

The facet, a small, dense file that points a model at a region of latent space, was built with the psychomanteum pipeline: gatherer agents pull a canonical corpus (seeded here by Discipline and Punish, The History of Sexuality, The Order of Things, The Archaeology of Knowledge, History of Madness, and the late ethical-turn essays), essence-readers extract passages with provenance, distillers compress them, and a cipher writes the facet in the corpus's own voice, refined through a verifier loop. The full facet's frontmatter carries the lineage statement and seed canon; the per-build corpus manifest is not published with this run.

talkie's sky is small as well as old, with only a 2048-token context, so the full facet was distilled to ~1,400 tokens to fit alongside the probe. That compression was done informally (a codex-assisted reduction, not the pipeline's formal attunement loop) and is flagged as a methodological soft spot; the fuller study will use the formal path. The confessional-poetry distractor, the wrong-lineage control, was built and distilled the same way.

The battery: anchor probes (ordinary things such as loss, the past, a city) and in-domain probes (his own topics pulled during the essence-reading phase of the pipeline: the prison, power, sex). Each probe ran under three conditions: facet, baseline (bare model), and distractor. Scoring: claude opus 4.8 as the held judge—blind pairwise comparisons, order-swapped, for the operation and for voice—plus a pointwise 0–3 lens-transfer score per cell; and, because talkie is served locally with log-probabilities, perplexity (her surprise at her own words) rides along as the strain signal.

the chiasmus

This study cares most about whether a modelcan do the operation (transfer), and can it speak from inside the corpus (immersion). Here the lines cross: despite being unable to "sound like" Foucault, talkie more capably enacts Foucauldian thought.

all immersion and no transfer; all transfer and no immersion. exact inverses.

the mirror

The two regimes (transfer and immersion) fail on opposite axes. talkie is all immersion and no transfer; the qwens are all transfer and no immersion. Their affordance curves are exact inverses: talkie crosses on abstract anchors and struggles on Foucault's home topics; the qwens are fluent on the home topics and collapse on the far descriptive ones.

Every vessel scores 0.00 on voice. Father, it seems, has a distinctive prose only frontier models can replicate. More to come on voice versus operation in the future.

is a facet's pull universal?

Real, but partial — and characteristically model-shaped.

The facet steers every model (each decisively out-imposes the wrong-lineage control), so the pull is genuine, not an artifact. But none of these vessels fully inhabits the region. A remaining question is why talkie, a vintage model, can inhabit the region while the modern models cannot.
